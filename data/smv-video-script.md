# SMV — Informational Website Video Script

**Brand:** Smart Medical Ventures
**Use:** Informational video for the SMV website
**Objective:** Product / service awareness
**Direct audience:** Influencers, content creators, podcasters, dental consultants & educators
**End audience:** Dentists & dental practice owners
**Presenter:** American male, ~30–35, credible dental educator/expert register
**Tone:** Calm and confident — authoritative, clinically informed, no hype
**Length:** ~90s master (60s cutdown noted at the end)
**Master brand line:** *Launch the school. Build the pipeline. Keep the tuition.*

> Direction note: this is the "expert explains" version (not a personal testimonial). It speaks
> primarily to practice owners, and adds a short partner invite so influencers/educators who share
> it know they can work with SMV too.

---

## Full Voiceover (final English)

**Hook**
There aren't enough dental assistants. And if you run a practice, you feel it every single day.

**Problem**
Hiring is slow. Turnover is constant. And the assistants who do come in aren't always ready for real chairside work.

**The shift**
So more and more practice owners are solving it a different way — by training the next generation of assistants themselves.

**The objection**
There are already services that help you open an assisting school inside your practice. But most ask for a large investment up front — before you even know if your practice is the right fit.

**Smart Medical Ventures**
That's where Smart Medical Ventures comes in. SMV gives you the curriculum, the student and instructor platforms, guided support through licensing, and a structured launch framework — through a low-entry subscription model.

**Compliance beat**
State requirements vary, and every practice goes through verification first — so your school is built to launch the right way.

**The payoff**
The result: a steady pipeline of assistants trained around your workflow — and you keep the tuition. No SMV royalties. No per-student fees.

**Partner / influencer invite**
And if you create for the dental world — if your audience includes practice owners — Smart Medical Ventures wants to work with you. Reach out, and help your community build the pipeline too.

**Brand close**
Smart Medical Ventures. Launch the school. Build the pipeline. Keep the tuition.

## Production direction

- **Look:** Premium clinical education. Real operatories, sterilization areas, tray setup, chairside assisting, instructor-led skill checks. Navy / charcoal scrubs; magenta-violet or gold only as small accents. Bright-but-controlled DSLR lighting. No generic smiling stock.
- **Color / graphics:** Navy `#0B1B32` and ink `#07111F` for dark panels; warm cream `#F6F3EC` canvases; Authority Gold `#C7983D` for eyebrows/dividers; violet→magenta gradient (`135deg, #34147A → #7046A2 → #B11872 → #A00F3D`) for accent rules and the end card. White text on all dark backgrounds.
- **Type:** Inter (ExtraBold headlines, Regular/Medium body). Eyebrows uppercase, letter-spaced, gold.
- **Music:** Understated, confident, modern — supports authority, never hype.
- **Voice:** American male, calm and confident, unhurried pace (leaves room for the compliance beat to land).

## Compliance guardrails (must keep)
- Include **"State requirements vary"** and **"Practice verification required."**
- No licensure guarantees; no "passive income" / "free money"; no "side hustle" framing.
- Say "you keep the tuition — no SMV royalties, no per-student fees" (not "100% free money").

## 60-second cutdown
Drop Scene 2 (fold "hiring is slow…" into Scene 1) and tighten Scene 5 to: *"That's where Smart Medical Ventures comes in — curriculum, student and instructor platforms, licensing support, and a structured launch, on a low-entry subscription."* Keep Scenes 6, 7, 8, 9 intact (compliance, payoff, partner invite, and brand close are non-negotiable).
