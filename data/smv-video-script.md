# SMV — 30-Second Awareness Commercial

**Brand:** Smart Medical Ventures
**Use:** Informational / awareness commercial for the SMV website
**Objective:** Product / service awareness
**Direct audience:** Influencers, content creators, podcasters, dental consultants & educators
**End audience:** Dentists & dental practice owners
**Presenter / narrator:** American male, ~30–35, credible expert register
**Tone:** Calm and confident — authoritative, clinically informed, no hype
**Length:** 30 seconds (5 scenes)
**Master brand line:** *Launch the school. Build the pipeline. Keep the tuition.*

> "Expert explains" version (not a personal testimonial). VO stays tight; the required compliance
> lines and the influencer/partner invite are carried as on-screen supers and the end card.

---

There aren't enough dental assistants — and every practice feels it.

Smart Medical Ventures. Launch the school. Build the pipeline. Keep the tuition.

*(~62 words ≈ 30s at a calm pace.)*

## Production direction
- **Look:** Premium clinical education — real operatories, sterilization, tray setup, chairside assisting, instructor-led skill checks. Navy/charcoal scrubs; gold or violet-magenta only as small accents. Bright-but-controlled DSLR light. No generic stock smiles.
- **Color:** Navy `#0B1B32` / ink `#07111F` dark panels; warm cream `#F6F3EC`; Authority Gold `#C7983D` eyebrows/dividers; gradient `135deg #34147A → #7046A2 → #B11872 → #A00F3D` for accent rules and the end card. White text on all dark backgrounds.
- **Type:** Inter (ExtraBold headlines, Regular/Medium body); eyebrows uppercase, letter-spaced, gold.
- **Music:** Understated, confident, modern — never hype.
- **Voice:** American male, calm and confident, unhurried.

## Compliance guardrails (must keep)
- Show **"State requirements vary"** and **"Practice verification required."**
- No licensure guarantees; no "passive income" / "free money" / "side hustle."
- "You keep the tuition — no SMV royalties, no per-student fees" (not "100% free money").
