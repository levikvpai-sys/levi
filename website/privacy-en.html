Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 November 2026

Who we are

levik.ai is operated by LEVIK Production, an AI film studio owned by Levi Itshak Cohen, Israel. We respect your privacy and follow the Israeli Protection of Privacy Law, 5741-1981, and its regulations. This policy explains what information we collect, why, and what your rights are.

Privacy questions: hello@levik.ai or +972 58-625-5511.

What we collect

  • Information you give us: when you use the contact form: name, phone, project type, message and, if you tick it, consent to receive updates.
  • WhatsApp, phone and email: when you contact us there, we receive what you send, such as your name, number and message.
  • Technical data: like every website, the servers hosting the site and videos receive basic technical data (IP address, browser and device type) to show you the page.

You are not required to give us any information, but without contact details we cannot reply.

How we use it

  • To reply, send a quote and run your project.
  • To send updates and new work, only if you explicitly agreed. You can withdraw at any time.
  • To operate, secure and improve the website.
  • To meet our legal obligations.

We do not sell personal information or use it for third-party advertising.

Cookies and browser storage

The site stores only two technical items in your browser: your choice in the cookie notice, and the fact that you have already seen the opening screen (until you close the browser). No analytics, advertising or tracking tools are installed. If we add analytics in the future, it will run only after you agree in the cookie notice.

Who we share it with

We use service providers that process information on our behalf, only as needed:

  • Vercel: hosting for the website and videos.
  • WhatsApp (Meta): if you choose to contact us on WhatsApp, the chat is also subject to WhatsApp's privacy policy.
  • Email and spreadsheet services (such as Google), to receive and manage form enquiries.

Some providers store data on servers outside Israel, with appropriate safeguards. We disclose information to authorities only when the law requires it.

Retention and security

We keep enquiries and client details for as long as needed to handle the enquiry, the project and our legal obligations, and then delete them. We use reasonable security measures, including encrypted connections (HTTPS) and limited access. No system is completely secure; if a security incident occurs we will act as the law requires.

Your rights

  • To see the information we hold about you.
  • To ask us to correct or delete it.
  • To stop receiving updates at any time, by replying "unsubscribe" or contacting us.

Requests: hello@levik.ai. We reply within 30 days. You may also contact the Israeli Privacy Protection Authority.

Minors and changes

The site is intended for adults; a parent or guardian should submit a minor's details. We may update this policy; the date at the top shows the latest version.
