Accessibility Statement

Last updated: 20 November 2026

Our commitment

LEVIK Production believes the website should be easy to use for everyone, including people with disabilities. We made the site accessible in line with the Israeli Equal Rights for Persons with Disabilities Law, 1998, and the Service Accessibility Regulations, 2013.

Accessibility level

The site follows Israeli Standard IS 5568, based on WCAG 2.0 level AA. It was tested in Chrome, Safari and Firefox on desktop and mobile.

What we did

  • Full keyboard navigation (Tab, Enter, Esc) with a clearly visible focus.
  • A "Skip to content" link at the top of every page.
  • Logical heading structure and standard HTML elements, so screen readers understand the page.
  • Text alternatives for informative images.
  • Colour contrast between text and background at level AA.
  • Text can be enlarged to 200% without losing content.
  • The device "reduce motion" setting is respected: animations are reduced or stopped.
  • Videos start muted. All background videos and animations can be stopped from the accessibility menu, and they do not play at all when the device "reduce motion" setting is on.
  • An accessibility menu on every main page (the round button in the corner): larger text, high contrast, highlighted links, readable font, text spacing, big cursor, and stop motion and video. Settings are remembered.
  • Each page declares its language and direction: Hebrew right-to-left, English and French left-to-right.
  • Clear labels for every form field.

Known limitations

  • Some films on the site are artistic pieces without dialogue, and some have no captions or audio description. We are adding captions to films with speech.
  • The opening screen is animated. You can skip it by scrolling, swiping or pressing any key, and it does not show again during the same visit.
  • External services such as WhatsApp are outside our control.

If you find something that is not accessible, please tell us and we will fix it.
