THE FOUNDER

Levi Itshak Cohen

Director, screenwriter and founder of LEVIK Production

The story is the star. The technology just works for it.

We start with the story: script, characters and direction. The technology comes after. That is how we create commercials, brand films and personal films that look like cinema, with no film crew, no actors and no location.

In 2026, “Josalle – The Holy Miser”, created by Levi Itshak Cohen and Gal Edri, won first place in the Israeli competition at the Tel Aviv AI Film Festival, out of 1,242 films from 80 countries.

Today we work with brands, businesses and families in Israel, France, the UK and the US, in Hebrew, English and French.

    FAQ

    Frequently asked questions.

    How long does it take?

    It depends on length and complexity. Most projects take anywhere from a few days to a few weeks. We'll give you an exact timeline in our very first conversation.

    Can I see how the film will look before ordering?

    Yes, with a ₪90 storyboard: key frames that show the style, characters and mood. Get yours

    How much does it cost?

    Every story is different, and so is every quote. After a short chat, you'll get a clear price.

    What should I send?

    The story, plus any photos, logo, names and key details you have. We'll handle the rest.

    Can you include real photos of the family or the business?

    Yes. We can build characters and places based on photos you send us.

    Who owns the rights?

    The film is yours to use. The exact terms are set out upfront in your quote.

    How many rounds of revisions are included?

    At least one round of revisions is always included. The exact number is in your quote.
