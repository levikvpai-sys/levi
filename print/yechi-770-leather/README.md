# יחי — 770 | Navy Leather Wall Piece — Production Files

**Trim (finished) size 304.8 × 609.6 mm (12 × 24 in)**, portrait.

**Production method: MACHINE EMBROIDERY directly into navy leather.** No patch, no appliqué, no glue, no print.
The 770 building was **redrawn as a sewable embroidery design**: 10 threads, clean shapes, satin columns for frames and trims, and a brick-pattern fill.
The **ready-to-sew machine files** (DST / PES / EXP) are in `output/embroidery/`.
Print files (PSD/PDF with 3 mm bleed) remain as an alternative or proof.

## 🇬🇧 English — embroidery production spec

### Files (`output/embroidery/`)
- **`770_EMB_FULL.dst / .pes / .exp`**: the whole piece in one file for large frames (279 × 490 mm, about 120 K stitches, 10 colours, gold last).
- **Three hoopings** for standard frames, each centred on its own design centre:

  | File | Size | Centre on the piece (from the trim's top-left) |
  |---|---|---|
  | `770_EMB_A_top-text` | 274 × 31 mm | (152, 88) mm |
  | `770_EMB_B_building` | 279 × 333 mm | (153, 295) mm |
  | `770_EMB_C_bottom-text` | 246 × 92 mm | (153, 516) mm |

- **`770_EMB_production-sheet.png`**: thread sequence with Madeira codes, stitch counts, placement diagram, leather settings (EN + 中文 + HE).
- **`770_EMB_stitch-preview*.jpg`**: rendered from the actual stitch data in the files.
- **`770_EMB_vector-artwork.svg/.pdf`**: 1:1 sewable artwork, one group per thread, for digitisers who want to edit in Wilcom, Hatch or Ink/Stitch.

### Threads (stitch order in B / FULL)
1. Madeira Classic Rayon 40 #1167
2. #1029
3. #1252
4. #1221 (brick)
5. #1091 (glass)
6. #1092
7. #1222 (stone)
8. #1173 (door)
9. #1009 (dark)
10. Madeira Metallic No.40 bright gold

Match by the physical colour card; on-screen colours are approximate. DST carries no colours, so follow the sequence on the sheet.

### Stitch construction
- Fills: tatami, 0.45–0.50 mm rows, edge-walk underlay (plus a light cross underlay on large areas), 0.12 mm pull compensation. Densities are already reduced for leather.
- Walls: one brick-red thread. Brick bond comes from open mortar rows and aligned joint penetrations.
- Satin columns: 0.40 mm, centre/zig-zag underlay, 1.4–5.5 mm wide.
- Gold text: satin-style metallic fill.
- No stacked layers: covered areas are knocked out.
- Moves are hidden under later stitching where possible, which brings it to about 630 trims for the full piece.
- Tie-in/tie-off on every start/stop.

### Leather
- 1.2–1.6 mm, firm.
- Cut-away stabiliser on the back.
- **Do not hoop the leather face**: float it on sticky backing or use a clamp frame (hoop burn is permanent).
- Needle 75/11–80/12 sharp/leather. For the metallic gold use a 90/14 metallic needle at slower speed.
- **Sew one test on the same leather first** and compare with the preview.

⚠️ **Hebrew reads right-to-left. Never retype the text.** Use these files as they are.

## 🇨🇳 中文 — 生产说明（请工厂阅读）

- **工艺：直接在深蓝色皮革上机绣。不要做贴布、不要胶粘、不要印刷。**
- **刺绣文件已经做好，可直接上机：** `770_EMB_FULL.dst`（大绣框，一次完成），或分三框：`A_top-text`、`B_building`、`C_bottom-text`（位置见生产单 `770_EMB_production-sheet.png`，以距成品左上角的毫米数表示）。
- 共 10 色：9 色 Madeira Classic Rayon 40 + 金色金属线（Madeira Metallic No.40）。DST 无颜色，请按生产单上的顺序换线。
- 约 12 万针。密度已按皮革降低（填充行距 0.45–0.50 mm，缎面 0.40 mm），无叠绣。
- 皮革 1.2–1.6 mm，背面加可剪衬。**不要用绣框直接夹皮面**（会留永久压痕），请用粘衬或夹框。
- 针：75/11–80/12 皮革针；金线用 90/14 金属线针并降低车速。
- **量产前请先在同样的皮革上打样**，并与效果图 `770_EMB_stitch-preview.jpg` 对照。
- ⚠️ **希伯来文从右向左书写，严禁重新输入文字。** 请直接使用这些文件。

### Rebuild / edit
```bash
pip install numpy scipy opencv-python-headless pillow psd-tools[composite] reportlab fonttools uharfbuzz pyclipper shapely pyembroidery
python3 build.py         # print files + PSD (about 5 min); also provides text outlines + leather
python3 embroidery.py    # sewable embroidery design + DST/PES/EXP + previews + sheet (about 1 min)
```
- The building redraw lives in `design()` in `embroidery.py`, in reference-image pixel coordinates. The text comes from `TEXT_LINES` in `build.py`.
- `source/threads/`: Madeira Rayon palette (Ink/Stitch, GPL-3).
- `fonts/ShofarDemi-Bold.ttf`: Culmus, GPL-2 with font exception.
