Locked rules — baked into every prompt:
1. Weather turn — the prologue and Scenes 1–3 play under storm or underground. From the instant the camera bursts out of the mine in Prompt 4a CUT 1, the sky is cloudless and golden and stays clear to the end.
2. No rivers, ever — no rivers, streams, creeks, lakes, ponds, wetlands or fish in any frame, the prehistoric forest included. All terrain is dry tundra, spruce, ridgelines and snowfields. This is a hard rule in the Global Style Prefix and in each prompt's Avoid line. The open Pacific appears only in the two strategic map/orbital views the script requires.
3. Character continuity — @Image1 is Kate in every prompt she appears in (1b, 2b, 5a, 7). Use the same reference image each time so her face never drifts. @Image3 is the coal seam and fossil leaf; keep the leaf identical between Prompt 1a and Prompt 3b — that match is the whole point of the film.

Scene:
One hundred million years ago, a warm Alaskan forest. No ice, no snow — a green world under a low warm sun. No people. The falling leaf is the only moving subject; every camera move is slow.

CUT 1 — wide establishing, 24mm, very slow push-in between the trunks:
Tall conifers and broad-leaved trees. Three hard shafts of sunlight fall between the trunks onto a dry forest floor of ferns, needles and fallen bark. Dust and pollen turn slowly inside the beams. Nothing moves but the light. The ground is dry — packed soil, leaf litter, no water anywhere.

CUT 2 — medium, 85mm, locked-off, camera follows the fall:
A single broad leaf releases from a branch at the top of frame and falls, turning slowly, catching a flash of gold on each rotation. It settles flat on the forest floor among the ferns.

CUT 3 — macro lens, very slow push-in:
The leaf on the dark soil, veins and edges razor-sharp, still lit warm. Fine soil begins to rise and close over it — not thrown, but rising gently as if time itself is covering it — until the frame goes almost entirely black with only a faint amber glow left at the leaf's center.

Avoid: no water, no rivers, no streams, no ponds, no snow or ice, no people, no animals, no text on screen, no logo, avoid jitter, avoid temporal flicker, avoid chaotic composition, keep the leaf shape identical across all three cuts.
