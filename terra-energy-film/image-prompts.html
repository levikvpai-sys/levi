The Light That Waited

Terra Energy Center — 60-Second Film · 20 Still-Image Prompts (one frame per beat)
Each prompt below generates one still frame — a keyframe, not a video shot. Written for any photoreal image generator (Midjourney, Nano Banana, Flux, Seedream, Firefly). The shared quality-and-rules tail is already appended to every prompt, so each one works standalone — just hit Copy and paste.

Aspect ratio: use 16:9 for all of them. For Kate’s frames (04, 07, 13, 16a): generate her character sheet first, then feed that image as a reference so her face stays identical across all four.
Locked client rules, already inside every prompt:
1. Weather turn — frames 01–09 are storm, night or underground. From frame 10 onward the sky is completely cloudless and golden, and stays that way to the end.
2. No water — no rivers, streams, lakes, ponds, wetlands or fish in any frame. All terrain is dry tundra, spruce, ridgelines and snowfields. The open Pacific appears only in frames 06b and 16b, where the script requires it.
3. No text in frame — every prompt forbids letters, numbers, logos and UI overlays. All titles and the Terra logo are added in post.
Shared quality & rules tail (already appended to every prompt) 
Photorealistic cinematic film still, 35mm anamorphic lens, natural depth of field, fine film grain, no CGI look, no 3D render, no illustration, no painterly style, no text, no letters, no numbers, no watermark, no logo, no UI overlay. Hard rule: no rivers, streams, creeks, lakes, ponds, wetlands or fish anywhere in frame.
