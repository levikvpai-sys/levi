The Light That Waited

Terra Energy Center — 60-Second Film · Director's Storyboard & Type System
RUNTIME 1:00 · 16:9 · 15 PANELS · 7 SCENES
Logline

A hundred million years ago, sunlight fell on an Alaskan forest and a single leaf carried it into the dark. Tonight, an operator watches her city lose its light — and the answer is the light that has been waiting under her feet the whole time.

The film is the journey of one thing: light. It goes down into the earth in the first ten seconds and comes back up for the rest of the film. Coal stops being a black rock and becomes stored sunlight — and the carbon returns to the ground that lent it. The earth gave us the light; we give back the carbon.

Locked client rules — apply to every panel
  1. Weather turn. Panels 1–9 play under storm or underground. From Panel 10 CUT 1 — the burst out of the mine — the sky is cloudless and golden and stays clear to the end. No storm cloud may reappear.
  2. No water. Ever. No rivers, streams, creeks, lakes, ponds, wetlands, or fish in any frame — the prehistoric forest included. All terrain is dry tundra, spruce, ridgelines and snowfields. The open Pacific appears only in Panels 6 and 15, where the script requires the strategic view.
  3. No text in frame. Every super and the logo are added in post. Nothing is rendered as on-set or in-camera type.
  4. Narration is locked. All eight approved VO lines appear here verbatim, in their approved positions.
Each panel shows the actual frame composition — horizon, subject placement and scale, camera move, and the color grade of that exact moment. Thirds guides are overlaid for framing reference only. Tick a scene when it's shot; progress saves in your browser. The full type system for every on-screen super is at the bottom of this document.

Typography System

Every super and the end card, specified so an editor can build the titles without guessing. All specimens below are rendering in the actual typefaces — what you see is what the film gets. If Terra Energy Center has a licensed brand typeface, it supersedes the display face below and everything else in this system (weights, tracking, case, position, timing) carries over unchanged.
