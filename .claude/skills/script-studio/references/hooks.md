# The Hook Specialist

The hook is the first **0–3 seconds**. It is the single highest-leverage part of
any short-form video: the first 3 seconds drive the majority of completion
variance, and if you lose ~60% of viewers there, the platform stops pushing the
video. A great body with a weak hook is a video no one sees.

> Rule: **The first line IS the hook.** No logos, no "hey guys," no slow build.
> Say it or show it instantly.

---

## The 4 Cognitive Triggers (every hook must fire ≥1, the best fire ≥2)

1. **Curiosity gap** — open a loop the brain *needs* closed. ("The reason your
   videos flop has nothing to do with editing.")
2. **Pattern interrupt** — break the visual/verbal expectation so the thumb stops.
   (Unexpected image, motion, sound, or a contradiction.)
3. **Self-relevance** — "this is about *me*." Call out the exact person/situation.
   ("If you're over 30 and still renting…")
4. **Emotional arousal** — shock, awe, anger, desire, fear, delight. High-arousal
   emotion drives shares.

Stack two in one sentence and you have a scroll-stopper. Example (curiosity +
self-relevance): *"You're seasoning chicken wrong — and it's why it's always dry."*

---

## The Two-Layer Hook (verbal + visual)

The strongest short-form hooks hit **two channels at once**:
- **Verbal hook** (spoken line or VO) — the promise/curiosity.
- **Visual hook** (first frame) — motion, a striking image, a mid-action moment,
  text-on-screen, or a "wait, what?" visual.
Design both. Never open on a static, low-information frame.

---

## 20 Named Hook Formulas (with fill-in templates)

Rotate between 5–10 of these; never use the same one every time — audiences
pattern-match and tune out.

1. **Contrarian Claim** — "Everyone tells you ___, but that's exactly why you're ___."
2. **Mistake Warning** — "Stop doing ___. It's quietly ruining your ___."
3. **List Tease** — "3 things I wish I knew before ___. Number 2 changed everything."
4. **Curiosity Gap** — "The real reason ___ is not what you think."
5. **Bold Promise / Result** — "Do this and you'll ___ in ___ days."
6. **Direct Call-Out** — "If you're a ___ who ___, watch this."
7. **Question Hook** — "Why do ___ always ___? Here's the answer no one says."
8. **Negativity / Warning** — "Never ___ before you ___ — here's what happens."
9. **Before/After Tease** — "This went from ___ to ___ in one step. Here's how."
10. **Story Cold-Open** — drop mid-action: "So I just ___ and it completely ___."
11. **Shock Stat** — "97% of ___ have no idea that ___."
12. **Insider Secret** — "___ don't want you to know this, but ___."
13. **Us vs Them** — "The difference between ___ who ___ and ___ who ___ is one thing."
14. **Time Machine** — "If I had to start ___ from zero, here's exactly what I'd do."
15. **Confession** — "I ___ for ___ years. Here's what I got wrong."
16. **Challenge / Dare** — "Bet you can't ___ without ___."
17. **Fear of Missing Out** — "You have until ___ before ___ changes forever."
18. **Visual Reveal** — open on the *result/end state* first, then rewind: "This is
    the ___. Now let me show you how."
19. **Fill-in-the-Blank / Poll** — "___ or ___? The answer surprises everyone."
20. **Unexpected Comparison** — "___ is basically ___ — and once you see it, you can't unsee it."

**Most consistently viral across niches right now:** the Contrarian Claim,
the Mistake Warning, and the List Tease — they stack curiosity + self-relevance +
a clear promise in one sentence. Confirm against live research per niche.

---

## Hook Craft Rules

- **≤14 words, ≤3 seconds.** If it doesn't fit, it's not a hook yet.
- **Front-load the payoff word.** Put the most charged word early ("*Broke.* Here's
  how I got out of it.").
- **Specific > vague.** "$4,300 in 9 days" beats "a lot of money fast."
- **Show the stakes**, not the setup. Skip context; earn it later.
- **No wind-up.** Cut the intro, the greeting, the "in this video."
- **Tension you'll resolve.** A hook is a promise — the payoff must pay it back, or
  you train viewers to distrust you (kills your channel long-term).
- **On-screen text** reinforces the spoken hook for the 70%+ who watch muted.

---

## After the Hook — hold the attention you just bought

The hook buys 3 seconds; **retention loops** keep them:
- **Open loops**: tease the payoff early, deliver late ("…but the third one is the
  real trick — get there in a sec").
- **Re-hooks every 5–10s**: mini pattern-interrupts (new shot, new question,
  visual change) to reset attention before it drops.
- **Escalate**: each beat should raise curiosity/stakes, never plateau.
- **Fast first cut**: change something visually within the first 1–2 seconds.
- **Pace to platform**: short-form cuts every 1.5–3s; don't let a shot idle.

---

## Research Protocol (do this per project — the user wants real research)

1. `WebSearch` the exact niche + "viral hooks / most viewed [topic] 2026" to see
   what's landing *now*.
2. Pull 3–5 current top-performing videos in the niche; write down their literal
   opening line + first frame.
3. Identify the **saturated** patterns (avoid the cliché) and the **rising** ones.
4. Draft 3–5 hooks using *different* formulas above, mapped to different triggers.
5. Pick one, keep the rest as alternates, and say *why* it wins.
