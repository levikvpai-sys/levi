# VFX & Effects — superhero, magic, destruction, sci-fi

The catalog of visual effects and how to describe them so they read as real and
cinematic. Descriptions are **prompt-ready** for AI video generation. (Modern superhero
films are ~95% VFX, ~2,500 shots — effects are a storytelling language, not decoration.)

## The vocabulary (know the difference)
- **SFX (practical)** — real, on-set: physical explosions, squibs, rain, fire, prosthetics.
- **VFX** — created/added in post: composited elements, digital environments, cleanup.
- **CGI** — computer-generated imagery: 3D characters, creatures, objects.
- **Best looks are hybrid** — practical base + digital enhancement reads most real.

## Effect categories (with how to describe them)
- **Super-powers / energy**: force blasts, telekinesis, super-speed streaks, force fields,
  electricity/lightning arcs, fire/ice manipulation, glowing eyes/hands. Describe the *source*,
  *color*, *motion*, and *effect on environment* (debris lifts, air distorts, light casts).
- **Explosions & fire**: fireball shape, shockwave, debris, smoke plume, heat haze, embers,
  the light it throws on faces and walls. Slow-mo + backlight = cinematic.
- **Destruction**: crumbling, shattering glass, collapsing structures, cracks racing across a
  surface, dust clouds. Particle debris + physics sell the scale.
- **Magic / spells**: glowing sigils, particle trails, energy tendrils, transmutation, portals,
  runes; give it a signature color + behavior per caster.
- **Transformations / morphs**: shapeshifting, aging, materializing/dematerializing, dissolving
  into particles/ash, liquid-metal, powering-up (the "surge").
- **Sci-fi / tech**: holograms, HUDs, force shields, hover, teleport shimmer, cloaking, neon UI,
  lasers, spaceships, robots/mechs.
- **Weather / environment**: magical storms, fog, aurora, embers-in-air, floating debris,
  god-rays, atmospheric particles (dust motes, snow, ash).
- **Creatures**: scale, weight, skin/fur detail, how light and environment interact with them.
- **Speed / time**: bullet-time freeze, speed-ramps, motion trails, time-reverse, phasing.

## Making VFX read as REAL (the craft)
- **Ground it in physics** — light spill, shadows, reflections, and debris must obey the scene;
  the effect should *interact* with the environment and the actor (wind, glow on skin, recoil).
- **Interactive light** — an energy blast lights faces and walls in its color; a fire flickers
  on everything near it. This is the #1 tell of believable VFX.
- **Motion blur & weight** — heavy things move heavy; fast things streak. Match the frame.
- **Practical anchor** — real smoke/sparks/wind on set, or referenced in the prompt, sells the CGI.
- **Restraint & story** — the effect serves a beat (the surge = her transformation); don't spectacle
  for its own sake.

## Shooting effects (camera + color)
- **Low angle + slow-mo** for power/scale; **push-in** on a charge; **whip-pan/crash-zoom** on impact.
- **Color**: the effect's signature hue should tie to the palette (see `cinematography-color.md`) —
  e.g. electric cyan power reading as the brand/hero color.
- **Backlight & atmosphere** (haze, particles) give effects volume and cinematic glow.

## Prompt-ready notation
`EFFECT: <named> | SOURCE: <from where> | COLOR: <hue> | MOTION: <how it moves> | INTERACTION: <light/debris/skin> | SHOT: <angle·move·speed>`
e.g. `EFFECT: energy surge power-up | SOURCE: erupting from her chest/eyes | COLOR: electric cyan | MOTION: rippling outward, hair and dust lift | INTERACTION: cyan light rakes her face and the walls, sparks trail | SHOT: crash-zoom to low-angle orbit, ramp to slow-mo.`
