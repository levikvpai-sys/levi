# Combat & Martial Arts — strikes catalog & how to shoot them

A precise catalog of combat sports/martial arts, their signature techniques, and the
camera language that sells impact. Move descriptions are **prompt-ready** for AI video
generation and fight choreography. (This is for choreography/filmmaking, not real-world harm.)

## Striking arts
- **Boxing** — **jab, cross, hook, uppercut, overhand**; defense: **slip, roll/weave, parry,
  bob, block**; footwork pivots. Clean, hand-only, head/body targets.
- **Kickboxing** — boxing + kicks: **roundhouse, front kick, side kick, push kick**.
- **Muay Thai ("art of 8 limbs")** — fists + **elbows** (horizontal, upward, spinning),
  **knees** (straight, flying), **teep** (push kick), low/mid/high **roundhouse**, **clinch**.
- **Karate** — linear **reverse punch (gyaku-zuki)**, **front kick (mae-geri)**, **side kick
  (yoko-geri)**, **knife-hand**, sharp kime (focus) and stances.
- **Taekwondo** — spectacular kicks: **spinning hook, tornado, jumping/flying, axe, back kick**.
- **Kung fu / Wushu** — flowing forms, circular blocks, animal styles, weapon forms, acrobatics.
- **Capoeira** — fluid, dance-like: **ginga** (base sway), sweeping kicks (**meia lua**),
  cartwheels (**aú**), evasions (**esquiva**).

## Grappling arts
- **BJJ (jiu-jitsu)** — takedowns to ground: **guard, mount, submissions** (armbar, triangle,
  rear-naked choke, kimura), sweeps.
- **Judo** — explosive **throws** (seoi-nage, o-goshi, uchi-mata), grip fighting, sweeps.
- **Wrestling** — **double/single-leg takedowns**, sprawls, slams, ground control.
- **MMA** — blends all: striking → **takedown → ground-and-pound → submission**; clinch work.
- **Krav Maga** — brutal practical combatives, simultaneous defense+attack (choreo caution).

## How to shoot fights (sell the impact)
- **Profile for hooks/round strikes** — a side angle hides the real gap between fist and target,
  so the strike reads as contact.
- **Frontal / over-the-shoulder for straight punches & front kicks** — makes the impact land.
- **Low angle** → power, dominance, size on the striker; **high angle** → the one taking damage.
- **"Cross the line"** — strike passes between lens and target → looks like a hit, no contact.
- **Impact energy**: quick **camera shake** on the hit, **snap/crash zoom** for a big blow,
  **speed ramp** (fast→slow→fast) on the decisive strike, a **1–2 frame cut** on contact for
  extra violence.
- **Wide to read choreography, tight for pain/reaction** — cut between the exchange (wide) and
  the face (CU). The reaction sells the hit as much as the strike.
- **Lens**: wide lens for immersive, aggressive close combat; telephoto compresses limbs so
  choreography looks tighter/more real.
- **Sound**: layered whooshes, impacts, grunts (mix in post, not the camera's job — but note it).

## Prompt-ready notation
`ART: <style> | MOVE: <named strike/throw> | TARGET: <head/body/legs> | SHOT: <size·angle·move> | IMPACT: <shake/zoom/speed-ramp>`
e.g. `ART: muay thai | MOVE: spinning elbow | TARGET: head | SHOT: profile MS, slight low angle, whip-pan follow | IMPACT: speed ramp + 2-frame cut + camera shake on contact.`
