# Vehicles & Car Cinematography — mounts, rigs & driving shots

A precise catalog of how to shoot people in/on/around vehicles. Descriptions are
**prompt-ready** for AI video generation and shoot planning.

## Exterior mounts (camera ON the car)
- **Hood / bonnet mount** — camera on the hood facing the driver through the windshield →
  the classic driving beauty shot / driver performance.
- **Door mount** — camera off the door, profile of the driver, road rushing behind.
- **Hostess tray** — a platform cantilevered off the door/window for driver/passenger coverage.
- **Low mount / insert car** — camera slung low near the road → dramatic low-angle speed,
  wheels, tarmac blur.
- **Suction cups (various positions)** — flexible small-camera rigs on glass/body panels.
- **Roof / rear mount** — following the road behind, or looking forward over the roof.

## Chase & motion rigs (camera on ANOTHER vehicle)
- **Camera / chase car** — a picture vehicle filmed from a following/leading camera car.
- **Russian Arm / U-Crane** — gyro-stabilized robotic crane arm on a chase car; swoops around
  a moving car in 3D (up, down, arcs, orbits) at speed → the premium car-commercial move.
- **Process trailer** — the picture car is towed on a low trailer; actors don't really drive
  (safe), real background parallax, room to rig lights and follow cameras.
- **Poor man's process** — car is static, motion faked with lighting/reflections/background
  plates → cheap interior dialogue driving.
- **Drone follow / aerial chase** — drone tracks the car for scope, speed, and reveals.

## Interior shots (camera INSIDE)
- **Driver POV** — the road ahead, hands on wheel → immersion.
- **Over-the-shoulder** — from the back seat past the driver to the road → conversation + motion.
- **Dashboard / windshield-mounted** — facing the driver for reaction/performance.
- **Passenger profile / two-shot** — side-on for dialogue between driver & passenger.
- **Detail inserts** — hand on gearshift, foot on pedal, key turn, wheel grip, speedometer, eyes
  in the rear-view mirror.
- **Snorricam / body mount** — rig on the driver; car spins around a fixed face → tension/panic.

## Angles & moves that make cars look cinematic
- **Low angle + wide lens** → speed, aggression, size; **top-down drone** → design & choreography.
- **Beauty pass**: slow parallax slider or Russian-arm orbit around a static/rolling hero car.
- **Wheel/tire detail with motion blur** → velocity; **reveal** the car with a crane-up or
  whip-pan; **push-in** on the driver's face for the emotional beat.
- **Reflections & light streaks** across the body sell premium; night city lights raking the
  paint is the luxury-ad staple.

## Prompt-ready notation
`SUBJECT: <driver/car> | RIG: <mount/rig> | SHOT: <size·angle> | MOVE: <parallax/orbit/tracking> | MOOD: <speed/luxury/tension>`
e.g. `SUBJECT: driver | RIG: hood mount | SHOT: MCU through windshield | MOVE: subtle handheld | MOOD: focused, night city lights raking the glass.`
