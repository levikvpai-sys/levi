# Movement & Physicality — gait, gesture, intimacy, blocking

How the body moves reveals character before a word is spoken. This covers ALL human
physical performance not handled by `combat.md` (fighting) or `dance.md` (dance):
walking, gesture, posture, romance/intimacy, everyday action, and staging. Descriptions
are **prompt-ready** for AI video generation and for directing performers.

## Why movement matters
- **Movement reveals character** — gait, posture, and habitual gestures tell us age,
  status, mood, confidence, and health instantly, without dialogue.
- **Motivate it** — a character moves because they want something; aimless motion reads false.

## Gait — the walk (a whole character in how they move)
Manipulate four dials to build any walk:
- **Pace** (slow/fast), **step length** (short/long stride), **weight** (heavy/light footfall),
  **posture** (upright/hunched, leading with chest vs head vs hips).
- **Reads:**
  - *Slow, heavy, hunched* → weariness, sadness, defeat, age.
  - *Quick, long, upright* → confidence, excitement, determination, purpose.
  - *Hesitant, short, small* → fear, uncertainty, submission.
  - *Loose, swaggering, hips-led* → cocky, sensual, dominant.
  - *Stiff, precise, controlled* → discipline, tension, authority, the military.
- **The hero walk**: grounded, unhurried, shoulders back, walking *toward* (into light/camera)
  — the empowerment staple. Shoot low-angle, tracking or push-in.

## Posture & gesture
- **Posture** (open/expansive = confidence, joy, power; closed/small = fear, shame, defeat) is
  the fastest emotional read on a body (ties to `emotions.md`).
- **Gesture** — hands and micro-movements: fidgeting (anxiety), still hands (control), open
  palms (honesty), pointing/chopping (aggression), self-touch (comfort/nerves).
- **Habitual action** — a repeated tic (adjusting a ring, cracking knuckles) makes a character
  specific and real.

## Romance & intimacy (the "love" domain)
Chemistry is built with *restraint and proximity*, not just contact:
- **The approach & distance**: closing space = desire/intimacy; holding a gap = tension/longing.
- **The almost-touch**: a hand hovering, a near-miss — anticipation is hotter than the touch.
- **Eye-line & lingering**: held gaze, glances to the lips, looking away and back.
- **Blocking a kiss/embrace**: the slow lean, the pause a breath apart, who initiates, the
  hand placement (jaw, waist, small of back). Shoot tight (CU/ECU), soft light, shallow focus.
- **Tension vs tenderness**: sharp, charged movement (want) vs slow, soft, melting movement
  (safety/love). Match camera: push-in on the turn toward each other.

## Everyday & functional movement
Sitting, standing, entering/exiting, working, eating, driving, waiting — do these *in character*
(a nervous man sits on the edge; a confident woman owns the whole chair). Enter late, move with
purpose; small realistic business grounds a scene.

## Blocking & staging (bodies in space)
- **Proximity = meaning**: close = intimacy or confrontation; distant = isolation, tension, authority.
- **Levels**: who's higher/lower encodes power (standing over a seated character).
- **Movement in the frame**: a character crossing to another shifts the power/relationship; turning
  away = rejection; stepping in = commitment.
- **Blocking with the camera**: block action so the camera move and the actor move work together
  (they turn *as* the camera pushes in).

## How to shoot movement
- **Full/wide** to read gait and whole-body language; **tight** for gesture and intimacy.
- **Track/gimbal** to travel with a walk; **push-in** on an emotional turn; **low angle** for
  the power walk; **slow-mo** to sell a hero entrance or a romantic beat.

## Prompt-ready notation
`MOVEMENT: <named> | QUALITY: <pace·weight·posture> | INTENT: <what they want> | SHOT: <size·angle·move> | MOOD: <...>`
e.g. `MOVEMENT: hero walk into arena | QUALITY: slow, grounded, shoulders back, chin up | INTENT: certainty | SHOT: low-angle tracking, slight slow-mo | MOOD: earned confidence.`
Romance: `MOVEMENT: the almost-kiss | QUALITY: slow lean, a breath apart, her hand hovering at his jaw | SHOT: tight CU, shallow focus, soft warm light | MOOD: aching anticipation.`
