# Dance — styles catalog & how to shoot them

A precise catalog of dance styles, their signature moves, and the camera treatment
that makes each look best. Move descriptions are **prompt-ready** for AI video
generation and choreography briefs.

## Street / hip-hop family
- **Breaking (b-boying/b-girling)** — 4 elements: **toprock** (standing footwork/entry),
  **downrock/footwork** (6-step, floor shuffles), **power moves** (windmills, flares,
  headspins, air flares, backspins), **freezes** (baby freeze, chair, air chair, handstand).
- **Popping** — muscle **hits**, the **wave** (body/arm ripple), **tutting** (geometric
  angles), **animation**, **robot**, **strobing**, **dime stops**.
- **Locking** — the **lock** freeze, **points**, **wrist rolls**, **Scooby-Doo**, funky and
  exaggerated, comedic energy.
- **Krump** — **chest pops**, **stomps**, **arm swings**, **jabs**, raw explosive emotion.
- **House** — **jacking** (chest ripple), fast intricate **footwork** (shuffles, skating),
  **lofting**, groove-driven and continuous.
- **Waacking** — fast **arm whips/rotations**, dramatic poses, to disco/soul.
- **Voguing** — the **5 elements**: hand performance, catwalk, duckwalk, spins & **dips**
  ("death drops"), floor performance; angular, precise, runway attitude.
- **Litefeet / flexing / shuffle / tutting** — regional street styles; fast footwork, bone-
  breaking illusions, cutting-edge shapes.

## Commercial / choreography
- **Commercial hip-hop / choreo** — clean, synced, camera-facing routines (music-video style).
- **K-pop** — precise formation changes, sharp unison "point choreography," hooks tied to a
  signature move per song.
- **Jazz funk** — sassy, sharp, stylized musicality.

## Afro / Caribbean / Latin
- **Afrobeats / Amapiano** — grounded bounce, **zanku/legwork**, shoulder isolations, gwara gwara.
- **Dancehall** — energetic, low, expressive named steps (e.g. willie bounce), attitude.
- **Latin & ballroom** — **salsa** (fast spins, shines), **bachata** (sensual hip motion),
  **tango** (sharp legwork, drama), **samba** (bounce), **cha-cha**, **waltz** (elegant sweep).

## Studio / concert
- **Contemporary / lyrical** — floor work, extensions, emotional fluidity, controlled falls.
- **Ballet** — turnout, **pirouettes**, **grand jeté** leaps, **arabesque**, precision & line.
- **Jazz / tap** — syncopation, rhythm; tap = percussive footwork the mic must catch.

## How to shoot dance (camera treatment)
- **Show the feet & full body** — use **full/wide shots** for footwork, formations, and power
  moves; a close-up that cuts the legs kills the dance.
- **Low angle** → power and height on jumps/freezes; **orbit/arc** → energy and 3D flow.
- **Slow-motion / speed ramps** → isolate a hit, a dip, a leap at its peak.
- **Cut ON the beat** — edit rhythm must lock to the music; whip-pans between formations.
- **Tracking/gimbal** to travel with movement; **top-down** for formations and floor work.
- **Match energy**: fast cuts for power/street, long flowing takes for contemporary/ballet.

## Prompt-ready notation
`STYLE: <style> | MOVE: <named move> | ENERGY: <explosive/fluid/sharp> | SHOT: <size·angle·move> | SPEED: <realtime/slow-mo>`
e.g. `STYLE: breaking | MOVE: windmill into freeze | ENERGY: explosive | SHOT: low-angle wide orbit | SPEED: ramp to slow-mo on the freeze.`
