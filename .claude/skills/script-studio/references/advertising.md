# Advertising — the perfect commercial, A→Z

Everything needed to build a converting ad from strategy to final frame. Structures are
**prompt-ready** and map onto the Micro-Beat pacing in `script-structure.md`.

## 1. Strategy first (before a single shot)
- **One insight**: the human truth the ad taps ("no one wants a drill, they want the hole").
- **Single-minded proposition**: the ONE thing to communicate. Cut everything else.
- **Objective**: brand (feeling/recall) vs performance (click/buy now)? It changes structure.
- **Audience & pain/desire**: who, and what keeps them up at night or lights them up.
- **The product as the hero**: it must be the answer to the tension you set up.

## 2. Ad structures (pick one, wrap in a strong hook)
- **PAS** — Problem → Agitate → Solution. Fast, reliable for pain-point products.
- **AIDA** — Attention → Interest → Desire → Action.
- **Before–After–Bridge (BAB)** — the pain now → the dream later → bridge = your product.
- **Hook–Story–Offer** — stop them → make them feel → ask.
- **Problem–Solution / Demo** — show the struggle, show the product solving it live.
- **Feature → Benefit → Meaning** — what it is → what it does → what it means for *them*.
- **Testimonial / UGC** — a real (feeling) person vouches; trust-driven, native to social.

## 3. Ad formats
UGC / talking-head · testimonial · product hero (beauty) · founder/brand story · unboxing ·
demo/how-to · before/after · comparison · lifestyle/aspirational · mascot/character ·
problem-agitation skit · "day in the life." Match format to platform and objective.

## 4. Anatomy by length
- **6s (bumper)** — one idea, brand + product + a single punch. No room for setup.
- **15s** — Hook (0–2s) → problem/desire (2–5s) → product as answer (5–11s) → CTA + brand (11–15s).
- **30s** — Hook → relatable problem → agitate → product solution/demo → proof → CTA + logo.
- **60s+** — room for a story arc (mini Save-the-Cat), emotional build, then the turn to product.

## 5. The non-negotiables of a great ad
- **Hook in ≤3s** (see `hooks.md`) — social ads live or die here; assume muted → on-screen text.
- **Product is the hero**, shown solving a real tension — never a features list.
- **One message.** Confusion kills conversion.
- **Show, don't claim** — demonstrate the benefit; specifics beat adjectives.
- **Emotion + logic** — feeling drives the share, a reason justifies the buy.
- **Clear single CTA** — one action ("shop now," "link in bio"), stated and shown.
- **Brand moment** — logo/name/color lands and is *remembered* (ideally tied to the payoff).
- **Native to platform** — UGC rawness for TikTok/Reels; polished hero for YouTube/CTV.

## 6. Research method (per campaign)
1. `WebSearch` current top-performing ads in the category + the brand's competitors.
2. Note the hook, structure, and CTA of 3–5 winners; find the saturated cliché to avoid.
3. Pull the platform's spec (length, aspect, safe zones) and creative best-practices.
4. Draft 2–3 concepts on different structures; recommend one with the reasoning.
