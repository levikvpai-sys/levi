# Camera Movement — the cinematic motion toolkit

Movement = emotion in motion. Where `camera-angles.md` picks the *frame*, this picks
how the frame *travels*. Every move must be motivated by the beat. Descriptions here
are written to be **prompt-ready** for AI video generators (e.g. Higgsfield's motion
presets) as well as live shoots.

## Ground / rig moves
- **Push-in (dolly-in)** — camera creeps toward the subject → rising tension, a dawning
  realization, intimacy. The single most emotionally reliable move. Prompt: *"slow dolly
  push-in on the subject's face."*
- **Pull-out (dolly-out)** — retreat → reveal context, abandonment, isolation, an ending.
- **Truck / crab** — camera slides left/right parallel to the subject → energy, reveal one
  side, follow lateral motion.
- **Pedestal / boom** — camera rises or lowers vertically without tilting → stature, reveal.
- **Pan / tilt** — pivot horizontally/vertically from a fixed point → scan, reveal, follow.
- **Crane / jib** — sweeping vertical arc → grandeur, scope, the "big" establishing or
  climactic move. Prompt: *"crane up to reveal the wide landscape."*
- **Tracking / following** — travel *with* a moving subject → urgency, immersion (the
  Steadicam corridor walk, the running follow).
- **Arc / orbit (360° / 180°)** — circle the subject → intensity, a "hero moment,"
  disorientation, or product showcase from all sides. Prompt: *"smooth 360° orbit around
  the subject, subject centered."*
- **Steadicam / gimbal** — floating smoothness through space → dreamlike, continuous flow.
- **Handheld** — organic shake → raw, urgent, documentary, chaos, immediacy.

## Special / signature moves
- **Dolly zoom (Vertigo / "the Hitchcock")** — dolly one way while zooming the other; subject
  stays same size, background warps → dread, vertigo, a mind-bending realization.
- **Crash / snap zoom** — sudden fast zoom in → punch, emphasis, comedy, meme energy.
- **Whip pan** — ultra-fast blur pan → energetic transition/cut, momentum, comedy.
- **Snorricam / body-mount** — camera rigged to the actor's body; world spins around a fixed
  face → disorientation, panic, intoxication.
- **Parallax slider** — short lateral move that separates foreground/background → depth,
  a "premium" product feel.
- **Roll / Dutch-in** — camera rotates toward a canted angle → unease building in real time.

## Aerial / drone (the "רחפן" moves)
- **Aerial orbit** — drone circles a point of interest → showcase a building/car/person from
  every side; the most-requested cinematic drone move.
- **Reveal (crane-up / tilt-up reveal)** — start low/tight, rise or tilt to unveil the vista.
- **Fly-through / FPV** — dive through a space (doorway, crowd, gap) → visceral, immersive,
  high-energy (FPV drone).
- **Top-down / bird's-eye (with rotation)** — straight down, often slowly rotating → pattern,
  scale, satisfying symmetry.
- **Fly-away / pull-back reveal** — retreat and climb from the subject → isolation, epic scale,
  a closing shot.
- **Tracking / chase (drone follow)** — drone follows a moving subject (car, runner) → speed,
  scope.
- **Push-in with gimbal tilt-down** — fly forward while tilting down to land attention on a
  ground detail.

## Choosing the move (Hollywood convention)
- Match the move to the beat: **push-in on truth/confession, handheld on panic, static on
  dread, crane/aerial on scale, orbit on the hero beat, whip-pan on a comedic cut.**
- **Motivate it** — camera moves because the story pushes it, not to show off.
- **One idea per move** — a shot that tries to do three things reads as noise.
- **Speed = emotion**: slow moves feel weighty/ominous or romantic; fast moves feel urgent,
  chaotic, or comedic.
- **Short-form**: front-load motion in the first frame; use snap-zooms/whip-pans on beats;
  keep something moving so no shot idles.

## Prompt-ready notation
`MOVE: <name> | speed: <slow/med/fast> | on: <beat> | subject stays: <centered/…>`
e.g. `MOVE: 360° orbit | speed: med | on: the reveal | subject centered, low angle.`
Map to AI-motion presets by name (orbit, dolly-in, crane-up, fly-through, whip-pan, dolly-zoom).
