# Mode specs

Replace `[sujet]`, `[niche]`, `[audience]` with the brand profile. `PASSION` = the creator's CTA keyword.

## §1 · Hooks
- 15 hooks on [sujet]: 5 contre-intuitifs, 5 erreur/peur, 5 qui ouvrent une mini-histoire.
- Max 10 words each, sayable in < 2 seconds.
- Rank strongest → weakest; one line on why the top 3 stop the scroll.

## §2 · Reel 30 secondes (~75 mots parlés)
Structure:
| Temps | Rôle |
|---|---|
| 0–2 s | Interruption de pattern : geste ou phrase inattendue |
| 2–12 s | Mini-histoire vécue avec un moment de bascule précis |
| 12–24 s | UNE seule leçon actionnable |
| 24–30 s | CTA PASSION qui découle de l'histoire |

Output as a table: **Temps | Ce que je dis | Texte à l'écran | Ce qu'on voit**.
Then silently re-read as a scrolling viewer: where do they drop? Fix it. Also give 2 alternative hooks.

## §3 · Idées virales
- 5 ideas in [niche] for [audience], each a different angle: série, tuto copiable, avant/après, erreur, coulisses.
- Per idea: hook exact, format (+ durée ou nb de slides), émotion visée, pourquoi on sauvegarde, pourquoi on partage.
- Drop anything seen 100 times in the niche.
- Output as a table.

## §4 · Description
- Line 1: < 12 words, contradicts a belief or creates tension (it's all that shows before « plus »).
- Then 3–5 short lines: expérience perso → leçon → phrase mémorable.
- End: invitation to comment PASSION that follows from the text. No hard sell.
- Give 3 alternatives for line 1 above the full caption.
- Optional: 3–5 niche hashtags at the end, never more.

## §5 · Carrousel
- Cover slide: max 8 words, a promise + curiosity.
- 7 tips: max 15 words each, start with an action verb, include one precise detail (number, exact word, tool). Nothing a beginner already read elsewhere.
- Final slide: CTA PASSION.

## §6 · Recyclage
Input: content the user pastes.
1. Central idea in one sentence.
2. Reel 30 s (§2 table format).
3. Carrousel 7 slides (cover + 5 value + CTA).
4. Post statique: visual line ≤ 12 words + short caption.
Each format gets a **different hook**. Keep the creator's voice.

## §7 · CTA
- 10 CTAs: 4 commentaire (2 with PASSION), 3 sauvegarde, 3 question qui lance la discussion.
- < 15 words each, each gives a reason to act. Group by type.

## §8 · Pack semaine
Deliver, in this order:
1. 3 best hooks (§1, top 3 only)
2. 1 Reel (§2)
3. 5 idées (§3)
4. 1 description (§4)
5. 1 carrousel (§5)
6. Recyclage of the Reel's idea into carousel + static (§6, skip the Reel part)
7. 10 CTA (§7)
8. Calendrier: Lun–Dim table, which piece goes out which day, format + CTA.
