## Benchmarks (21 Reels, Apr–Sep 2026; 67 followers at audit)
- Average: ~9.4 likes, ~3 comments per Reel.
- Best hook pattern: "X לא קיים / לא צולם" + a question at the end.
- Best comment drivers: emotional either/or question ("איזו מהן נגעה בכם?" — 12 💬) and keyword CTAs ("מדריך", "פרומפט").
- Weakest: pure "contact us" posts (0 comments), duplicate uploads, mixed languages, captions sending people to YouTube/Higgsfield.
- Insights API unavailable under 1,000 followers — track likes, comments, saves, shares, DMs manually.
